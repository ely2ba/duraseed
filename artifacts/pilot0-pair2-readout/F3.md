## Definitions

Raw Pass@1 is exact successes divided by all stored draws. Pass@k is the
item-averaged unbiased estimator using the stored 16 draws. Syntactic invalidity
is the recorded `not valid_syntax` field. A length stop means sampled tokens
reached the 4,096-token cap. The registered loop detector is applied only to
length-stopped completions. Unique completions are exact text identities.
Verified strategy-family counts include reward-1 completions only. Surprisal is
the pooled negative sampled-token log probability, not full-vocabulary entropy.
The full item, family, failure-code, and posterior-cover records remain in the
source JSON linked above.
